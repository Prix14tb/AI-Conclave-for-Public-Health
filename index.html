Academic Events

The National Conclave features high-impact academic forums designed to foster interdisciplinary innovations. The event includes an Idea-Pitching Contest for AI-based models in public health,
alongside competitive Research Paper Presentations (Oral & Poster).

Idea Pitching Contest

Present innovative AI conceptual layouts and working prototypes designed to address frontline public health challenges.

Research Paper Presentations

Showcase original research, systematic reviews, and analytical models through oral papers and digital poster presentations.

Focus Tracks & Domains

Key areas covered across Academic Events

Note: Participants are welcome to make submissions in any relevant public health & AI domains other than the focus domains listed below.

Diagnostics & Digital Health

Applied epidemiology, point-of-care tools, and AI-driven diagnostics in Public Health.

Climate Change & Human Health

Environmental health, Predictive modeling, Climate adaptation strategies.

Outbreaks & Disaster Risk Reduction

Epidemiological surveillance, emergency response systems, and community resilience.

One Health and Antimicrobial Resistance

AI-model based interventions for addressing antimicrobial resistance through One Health approaches.

Medical and Public Health Education

Transforming public health education and training workflows in the AI-era.

Health Promotion and Communication

Effective communication using AI models, enhancing workplace and industrial safety.

Idea Pitching Contest Instructions

  • Team Size: Teams of 3 members (interdisciplinary teams preferred).
  • Abstract Submission: Submit a 300-word layout of your idea (Problem expected to be addressed, brief description of AI model, pilot results (if applicable)).
  • Preliminary Round: Initial presentation/pitch evaluation by expert jury.
  • Final Round: Working prototype/model demonstration.
  • Exciting awards and prizes for winners in each category.

Paper Presentation Instructions

  • Categories: Oral Research Presentations and Scientific Poster Showcases.
  • Abstract Format: Structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion) up to 250 words.
  • Review Process: Peer-reviewed selection by the Scientific Committee.
  • Publication: Accepted abstracts will be published in the conclave souvenir proceedings.
  • Best Paper and Best Poster awards presented during valedictory session.
Hands-on Workshop

Navigating AI for Medical Research

  • Prompt Engineering: Best practices for precise query design in research.
  • Fair Use of AI: Declarations, bias mitigation, and ethical considerations.
  • Brainstorming & Literature: Conceptualizing research ideas and summarizing evidence.
  • Statistical Analysis: Leveraging AI tools for data interpretation and models.
  • Editing & Proofreading: AI-assisted manuscript formatting and refinement.
  • Hands-on Practice: Live dataset analysis and practical exercises.
