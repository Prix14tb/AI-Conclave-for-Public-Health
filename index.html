Pre-Conference Workshop (14 December 2026)

Navigating Artificial Intelligence

for Medical Research

Comprehensive masterclass for clinicians, healthcare researchers, scholars, students and residents

Prompting & Evidence Synthesis

  • Prompt Engineering: Best practices for framing research questions.
  • Evidence Retrieval: Summarising dense biomedical literature rapidly.
  • Brainstorming Ideas: Designing hypothesis-driven scientific methodologies.

Analysis, Ethics & Governance

  • Statistical Interpretation: Interpreting outputs, graphs, and epidemiological trends.
  • Fair Use & Ethics: Declaring AI use, mitigating demographic bias, and ensuring reproducibility.
  • Scientific Writing: Formatting, editing, and adhering to international journal standards.

Academic Events

(15 & 16 December 2026)

Showcase your research and transform concepts into deployable public health models.

Idea Pitching Contest

Pitch AI-based conceptual frameworks or working prototypes for pressing community challenges.

  • • Teams of 3 members.
  • • Submit a 300-word structured concept note (Problem to be addressed, brief description of AI model, pilot results (if applicable))
  • • Preliminary round: Initial presentation/pitch evaluation by expert jury
  • • Final round: Prototype demonstration & jury presentation.
Exciting Prizes and Awards for winners

Research Presentations

Oral and Poster presentations.

  • • Structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion) up to 300 words
  • • Peer-reviewed selection by the Scientific Committee.
  • • Accepted submissions will be published in the conclave souvenir.
Best Oral Paper and Poster Awards will be presented during valedictory

Abstract submission deadline: 15 November 2026.
